How it works

A complete training system in your pocket

From a structured multi-week plan to a workout you generate in seconds — LifeSpike guides every rep and remembers every session.

Structured Programs

Follow a plan that progresses with you

Pick a goal and follow a 4-to-12-week program built around progressive overload. Every week and day is laid out — just press play.

  • Kettlebell, dumbbell & bodyweight tracks
  • Clear week-by-week progression
  • Your completion is saved automatically
Guided Workouts

Step-by-step demos with timed intervals

Each move comes with a visual demo, work and rest timers, and one-tap pause and skip — so you stay focused on training, not your screen.

  • Animated form demos for every exercise
  • Automatic work / rest countdowns
  • Voice and sound cues keep you on pace
Progress Tracking

See every gain — streaks, minutes & milestones

Your workouts, training time, and calories are tracked on-device. Build streaks, unlock achievements, and watch your weekly volume climb.

  • Weekly activity & streak insights
  • Achievement badges to chase
  • Export all your data anytime
